# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 26, 2026

## Overview

Perimenopause TR ("the App") is a health tracking application designed to help women monitor and document perimenopause symptoms. This Privacy Policy describes how the App collects, uses, and protects your information.

## Data We Collect

### Information You Provide

The App collects only the information you enter directly into the application:

- **Symptom entries:** Type of symptom (hot flash, night sweat, mood, sleep, energy, period), severity rating, and optional notes
- **Date information:** The date associated with each symptom record
- **App settings:** Language preference and other settings you configure

We do **not** collect your name, address, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.

### Automatically Collected Information

The App does **not** collect:

- Device identifiers (IMEI, serial number, MAC address)
- Location data
- Contact information
- Browsing history
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Advertising identifiers

## How We Use Your Information

Your data is used **exclusively** to provide the features of the App:

- Display your symptom history and trends
- Generate doctor reports for export
- Persist your data across app sessions
- Apply your language and display preferences

We do **not** sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties for any purpose, including advertising or marketing.

## Data Storage and Security

All data entered into the App is stored **locally on your device** using `AsyncStorage` (React Native's local storage). Your data is:

- **Never transmitted** to our servers or any third-party service
- **Never uploaded** to the cloud by the App
- **Protected** by your device's built-in security (biometrics, passcode, encryption)
- **Under your control** — you can delete your data at any time by clearing the app's storage

## Third-Party Services

The App does **not** integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data leaves your device.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the App and on this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
